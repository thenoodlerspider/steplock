StepLock

StepLock is a simple tool designed to help you build a healthier relationship with your phone. Instead of relying on sheer willpower to stop scrolling, StepLock connects your screen time directly to your physical activity. If you want to use your favorite apps, you have to earn the time by walking.
What It Does

StepLock blocks the apps you find most distracting. To unlock them, you must reach a physical step goal. You decide the exchange rate. For example, you can set it so that taking 100 steps earns you 1 minute of screen time. When your earned time runs out, the restricted app locks again until you get back on your feet.
How It Improves Your Life

Physical Health
Sitting for hours is easy when you are lost in a feed. StepLock interrupts that cycle. By linking your digital entertainment to physical movement, you naturally increase your daily step count. It encourages you to get off the couch, improve your daily activity levels, and build a consistent walking habit just by going about your normal digital life.

Mental Wellbeing
Mindless scrolling drains your focus and can leave you feeling fatigued. StepLock acts as a physical boundary for doomscrolling. When an app locks, it pulls you out of the screen and gives your brain a mandatory pause. The physical act of walking helps clear your mind, reduces digital fatigue, and makes you far more intentional about how and when you consume content.
Key Features

    Activity Driven Unlocking: Choose exactly which apps to restrict and set a custom ratio of steps to screen time.

    Usage Analytics: View clear breakdowns of how much time you spend on your phone and which apps consume most of your day.

    Trophies and Motivation: Earn experience points and badges like Focus Master or Digital Detox as you successfully manage your restricted apps.

    Customizable Limits: Set specific daily step goals and easily track your weekly average progress.
